# Nimbus Coffee Co. — Employee Handbook

Nimbus Coffee Co. is a fictional company. This handbook exists so ragmate has facts that no model
could know without reading it: ask about them and check whether the answer cites this file.

## Working hours

The core hours at Nimbus are 10:00 to 15:00, Monday to Thursday. Outside the core hours, everyone
plans their own day. Fridays are "slow Fridays": no meetings are scheduled before 13:00, and the
afternoon is reserved for the weekly demo at 15:30 in the Roastery room.

## Vacation and leave

Every full-time employee gets 27 vacation days per calendar year, plus the public holidays of the
country they work in. Part-time employees get vacation days in proportion to their contract.

Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days expire on
31 March; after that date they are lost. Requests for more than 10 consecutive days must be made at
least 6 weeks in advance through the HR portal, which is called "Brewhouse".

Parental leave is 16 weeks at full pay for every parent, which can be split into at most three
blocks within the child's first two years.

## Expenses

Travel by train is preferred for any journey under 5 hours. Hotel costs are reimbursed up to 140
euros per night. Meals while travelling are covered up to 45 euros per day, alcohol excluded.
Expense claims are filed in Brewhouse within 30 days, with a photo of every receipt.

## Equipment

New employees choose a laptop on their first day: a 14-inch MacBook Pro or a Lenovo ThinkPad X1.
Every employee also gets a yearly home-office budget of 350 euros, for example for a chair or a
monitor. Lost or broken equipment is reported to the IT desk, nicknamed "the Grinders".

## Coffee

Each office has two espresso machines. The house blend is called "Cumulus" and is roasted every
Tuesday. Employees can take home one 250-gram bag of Cumulus per week, free of charge.
